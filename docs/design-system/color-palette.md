# Color Palette

## Purpose

The Skyview color palette is inspired by the apartment itself.

Rather than following design trends, every color is extracted from the materials, textures and atmosphere experienced by our guests.

The goal of the palette is to create a feeling of calm, warmth and intimacy.

Our guiding principle is simple:

> Every color should help guests unwind.

---

# Design Principles

Our palette should always feel:

- Calm
- Warm
- Elegant
- Timeless
- Natural

It should never feel:

- Cold
- Corporate
- Futuristic
- Loud
- Overly luxurious

---

# Material Inspiration

The visual identity of Skyview is built around five recurring materials found throughout the apartment.

| Material | Brand Color |
|----------|-------------|
| Linen & Towels | Warm Ivory |
| Natural Oak | Warm Oak |
| Velvet | Signature Plum |
| Dark Metal & Architecture | Deep Graphite |
| Ambient Lighting | Champagne Glow |

# Primary Palette

## Warm Ivory

### Role

Primary Surface

### Inspiration

- Bed linen
- Bathrobes
- Towels
- Jacuzzi shell
- Soft daylight

### HEX

`#F6F3F1`

### Used for

- Page backgrounds
- Cards
- Large sections
- Forms

---

## Warm Oak

### Role

Natural Accent

### Inspiration

- Flooring
- Furniture
- Wooden wall slats
- Kitchen
- Wellness area

### HEX

`#C99A70`

### Used for

- Decorative elements
- Illustrations
- Dividers
- Warm accents

---

## Signature Plum

### Role

Primary Accent

### Inspiration

- Upholstered bedroom walls
- Decorative cushions
- Bedroom identity

### HEX

`#7A6274`

### Used for

- Primary buttons
- Active navigation
- Links
- Icons
- Highlights

Avoid using it for large page backgrounds.

---

## Deep Graphite

### Role

Primary Dark

### Inspiration

- Kitchen cabinetry
- Exterior walls
- Wellness architecture
- Metal details

### HEX

`#3F3B3A`

### Used for

- Footer
- Navigation
- Hero overlays
- Dark sections

---

## Champagne Glow

### Role

Premium Accent

### Inspiration

- Ambient lighting
- Welcome prosecco
- Evening atmosphere

### HEX

`#E6C7A6`

### Used for

- Small highlights
- Decorative details
- Premium accents

Never use for body text.

---

## Sage Green

Inspired by the terrace furniture.

---

## Aqua Spa

# Things to Avoid

- Pure black (#000000)
- Pure white (#FFFFFF)
- Neon colors
- Bright blue accents
- Heavy gradients
- Oversaturated colors

---

# Emotional Goal

When someone visits Skyview, the colors should communicate:

- Calm
- Warmth
- Privacy
- Comfort

Before they read a single word.
