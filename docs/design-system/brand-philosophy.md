# Design Principles

## Calm over complexity

Every interface should reduce cognitive load.

---

## Atmosphere before information

Emotion comes before functionality.

---

## Photography is the hero

The apartment tells the story.

The interface supports it.

---

## White space is part of the design

Don't fill empty space.

Let the content breathe.

---

## Authenticity over trends

Never follow trends if they don't fit Skyview.

---

## Unwind is the final goal

Every decision should help guests slow down.
