# Logo Usage

## Purpose

The Skyview logo is more than a visual identifier.

It is the first expression of the brand.

It should communicate the same feeling as the apartment itself:

- Calm
- Warmth
- Privacy
- Elegance

The logo should never feel louder than the experience it represents.

---

# Design Principles

The logo should be:

- Simple
- Timeless
- Elegant
- Memorable
- Minimal

It should avoid unnecessary decorative elements.

---

# Brand Inspiration

The logo is inspired by the essence of Skyview rather than its amenities.

It represents:

- The feeling of unwinding
- Calm architecture
- Warm hospitality
- Space and openness
- The connection between sky, water and home

The jacuzzi and sauna are part of the experience, not the identity.

---

# Logo Variations

The logo should exist in the following versions:

## Primary Logo

Full horizontal version.

Used in most situations.

---

## Stacked Logo

Vertical composition.

Used where horizontal space is limited.

---

## Icon

Simplified mark.

Used for:

- Website favicon
- Social media profile
- Mobile applications
- Small UI elements

---

## Monochrome Logo

Single-color version.

Available in:

- Deep Graphite
- Warm Ivory

---

# Clear Space

A minimum clear space should surround the logo at all times.

Use the height of the logo symbol as the minimum spacing.

No text or graphic elements should enter this area.

---

# Minimum Size

The logo should always remain readable.

Recommended minimum sizes:

| Context | Minimum Width |
|----------|--------------:|
| Print | 25 mm |
| Digital | 120 px |
| Favicon | Use icon version only |

---

# Color Usage

Preferred logo colors:

- Deep Graphite
- Warm Ivory

Signature Plum may be used selectively for premium applications.

Avoid using decorative gradients.

---

# Backgrounds

Preferred:

- Warm Ivory
- White
- Deep Graphite
- High-quality photography with sufficient contrast

Avoid:

- Busy backgrounds
- Low contrast
- Strong patterns
- Bright saturated colors

---

# Incorrect Usage

Never:

- Stretch the logo
- Rotate it
- Add shadows
- Add outlines
- Change proportions
- Change typography
- Add effects
- Use multiple colors without purpose

The logo should always remain clean and timeless.

---

# Tone

The logo should feel understated.

Its role is to support the brand, not dominate it.

Luxury should be perceived through restraint.

---

# Future Applications

The logo should work consistently across:

- Website
- Mobile devices
- Airbnb
- Booking.com
- Social media
- Business cards
- Welcome book
- Towels
- Bathrobes
- Signage
- Packaging
- Guest amenities

---

# Design Checklist

Before approving a logo variation, ask:

- Is it instantly recognizable?
- Does it remain legible at small sizes?
- Does it feel timeless?
- Does it reflect the Skyview brand?
- Does it communicate calm rather than luxury?
- Would it still feel appropriate ten years from now?

If the answer to any of these questions is "no", the design should be reconsidered.
