# Brand Guidelines

## Brand Personality

Skyview is:

- Calm
- Warm
- Elegant
- Human
- Timeless

Skyview is never:

- Loud
- Flashy
- Corporate
- Cold
- Trendy

---

## Voice & Tone

Our communication should always feel:

- welcoming
- relaxed
- personal
- respectful
- effortless

Never:

- salesy
- pushy
- overly formal

---

## Brand Values

- Authenticity
- Hospitality
- Privacy
- Simplicity
- Care

---

## Brand Promise

Every guest should leave feeling better than when they arrived.

---

## Guest Experience

We don't sell accommodation.

We create moments.

Every interaction should reduce stress.

Every detail should communicate care.

---

## Communication Principles

Speak like a thoughtful host.

Not like a hotel chain.

Keep messages:

- short
- warm
- clear
- human

---

## Writing Style

Prefer:

"We look forward to welcoming you."

Instead of:

"Your reservation has been confirmed."

Prefer:

"Take your time."

Instead of:

"Check-out is at 10:00."

Whenever possible,

make people feel welcomed,

not processed.

---

## Visual Consistency

Every visual element should feel:

- warm
- clean
- natural
- spacious

Avoid:

- visual noise
- unnecessary decoration
- stock-photo aesthetics

---

## Brand Check

Before publishing anything ask:

Does this feel like Skyview?

Does it help guests unwind?
