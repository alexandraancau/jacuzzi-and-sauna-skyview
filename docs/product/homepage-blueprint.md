# Homepage Blueprint

## Goal

The homepage has one primary objective:

Help visitors confidently decide to book Skyview.

The homepage should answer every important question before visitors feel the need to ask it.

---

# Success Criteria

After scrolling through the homepage, visitors should:

- understand what makes Skyview unique;
- trust the property;
- imagine themselves staying there;
- feel ready to check availability.

---

# Story Structure

The homepage should tell a story.

Not present information.

The story follows this journey:

Curiosity

↓

Trust

↓

Imagination

↓

Confidence

↓

Booking

---

# Section 01 — Hero

## Purpose

Create an emotional first impression.

## Visitor Question

"What is this place?"

## Content

- Large hero photography
- Headline
- Supporting paragraph
- Primary CTA

## Goal

Make visitors stop scrolling.

---

# Section 02 — Why Skyview

## Purpose

Explain why Skyview is different.

## Visitor Question

"Why should I stay here instead of somewhere else?"

Possible highlights:

- Private Jacuzzi
- Private Sauna
- Spacious apartment
- Premium comfort
- Self check-in

---

# Section 03 — The Apartment

## Purpose

Present the apartment.

Focus on atmosphere.

Not specifications.

Include:

- Living room
- Bedrooms
- Kitchen
- Terrace

---

# Section 04 — Wellness Experience

## Purpose

Sell the feeling.

Not the jacuzzi.

Not the sauna.

Explain what guests experience.

---

# Section 05 — Amenities

## Purpose

Remove uncertainty.

Examples:

- Wi-Fi
- Parking
- Coffee
- Air conditioning
- Fully equipped kitchen
- EV charger

---

# Section 06 — Gallery

## Purpose

Allow visitors to imagine their stay.

Large photography.

Minimal interface.

---

# Section 07 — Reviews

## Purpose

Build trust.

Use authentic guest reviews.

---

# Section 08 — Location

## Purpose

Help visitors understand where they will stay.

Include:

- Interactive map
- Nearby attractions
- Airport
- City centre

---

# Section 09 — Booking

## Purpose

Convert interest into action.

The booking section should feel simple.

Never overwhelming.

---

# Footer

Contains:

- Contact
- Address
- Social links
- Policies
