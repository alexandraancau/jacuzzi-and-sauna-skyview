# User Experience

## Purpose

The Skyview experience is designed to feel effortless.

Guests should spend their time imagining their stay—not figuring out how the website works.

Every interaction should reduce uncertainty and increase confidence.

---

# Experience Principles

The experience should always feel:

- Calm
- Intuitive
- Trustworthy
- Human
- Effortless

Whenever possible:

- Reduce decisions.
- Remove friction.
- Build trust.
- Let the content breathe.

---

# Experience Goals

Every visitor should be able to:

- Understand what Skyview offers.
- Trust the brand.
- Imagine themselves staying there.
- Find the information they need quickly.
- Complete a booking without confusion.

The website should never feel complicated.

---

# Guest Experience Journey

## 1. Discovery

The guest discovers Skyview.

### Emotion

Curiosity.

### Questions

- Is this place different?
- Is it worth my time?

### Need

Trust.

The first impression should immediately communicate authenticity.

---

## 2. First Visit

The guest opens the website.

### Emotion

Interest.

### Need

Instant calm.

The website should feel slower than the rest of the internet.

It should immediately create space to breathe.

---

## 3. Exploration

The guest browses the photography.

### Emotion

Imagination.

### Need

"I can already picture my weekend here."

Photography should tell the story before the text does.

---

## 4. Evaluation

The guest explores amenities, location and details.

### Emotion

Confidence.

### Need

Nothing important is missing.

Every answer should be easy to find.

---

## 5. Booking

The guest decides to book.

### Emotion

Excitement.

### Need

No friction.

Booking should feel obvious.

Not like completing paperwork.

---

## 6. Arrival

The guest arrives.

### Emotion

Relief.

### Need

Everything matches expectations.

Reality should reinforce the trust created online.

---

## 7. Departure

The guest leaves.

### Emotion

"I don't want to leave."

### Need

Take home a memorable experience.

Guests should remember how they felt more than what they saw.

---

## 8. Reflection

The guest writes a review.

### Emotion

Gratitude.

### Need

Share the experience with others.

Positive reviews should happen naturally because the experience exceeded expectations.

# UX Principles

## Reduce uncertainty

Every screen should answer the next question before the guest needs to ask it.

---

## Build trust early

Use:

- Authentic photography
- Transparent pricing
- Clear contact information
- Honest descriptions
- Real guest reviews

---

## Remove friction

Avoid unnecessary steps.

Only ask for information that is genuinely required.

Every interaction should feel natural.

---

## Keep the experience calm

The interface should never rush the guest.

Avoid:

- Popups
- Autoplay videos
- Aggressive banners
- Countdown timers
- Dark patterns

---

## Let photography lead

Photography is the primary storytelling tool.

The interface exists to support it.

---

## Mobile First

The majority of guests will first discover Skyview on their phone.

The mobile experience is the primary experience.

Desktop enhances it—it does not redefine it.

---

# Success Criteria

A successful experience means:

- Guests immediately understand what Skyview offers.
- Guests trust the website within the first minute.
- Information is easy to find.
- Booking feels simple and effortless.
- Every interaction supports the feeling of unwinding.

---

# Experience Checklist

Before publishing any page, ask:

- Does this reduce uncertainty?
- Does this build trust?
- Does this feel calm?
- Is the next step obvious?
- Does this help guests unwind?

If the answer to any question is "no", the experience should be reconsidered.
