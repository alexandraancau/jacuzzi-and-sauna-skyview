# Product Vision

## Vision

To become the first choice for travelers looking for a premium relaxation experience in Cluj-Napoca.

## Mission

To create an elegant and intuitive booking experience that helps guests discover the apartment, check availability and book directly with confidence.

## Success Metrics

- Achieve a 20% conversion rate from website visitors to confirmed direct bookings.
- Increase the number of direct bookings month over month.
- Reduce dependency on third-party booking platforms.
- Provide a booking experience that is simple, fast and trustworthy.

## Target Audience

### Primary Audience

- Couples (25–45 years old)
- Looking for a premium relaxation experience
- Interested in wellness, privacy and comfort
- Short city breaks and weekend getaways

### Secondary Audience

- Families with children
- Friends
- Business travelers
- Guests celebrating birthdays, anniversaries or proposals

## Unique Selling Proposition

We created the kind of place we always wished to find when we travelled.

Every detail has been carefully chosen based on our own experiences as guests—from the private jacuzzi and sauna to the fresh coffee, complimentary wine, fluffy bathrobes and small thoughtful touches that make every stay feel warm, relaxing and personal.

## Brand Values

- Hospitality before luxury
- Attention to detail
- Thoughtful comfort
- Authentic care
- Calm and relaxation


## Brand Personality

Our brand feels like a thoughtful host.

She welcomes guests with a genuine smile.
She is calm, warm and attentive.
She never feels loud or overwhelming.
She gives people space to relax while making sure they have everything they need.

Her confidence comes from care, not from showing off.

## Visual Direction

The website should feel calm, warm and refined.

It should use natural light, generous spacing, soft neutral colors and large, authentic photography.

The visual experience should never feel loud, crowded or overly luxurious.

Every design choice should support relaxation, trust and thoughtful hospitality.

## Visual Principles

We avoid:

- Loud colors
- Heavy visual effects
- Excessive animations
- Crowded layouts
- Generic luxury imagery
- Overly promotional language

We prefer:

- White space over decoration
- Authentic photography over stock images
- Simplicity over complexity
- Warmth over perfection

## North Star

Our North Star is one word:

UNWIND is not a slogan.

It is the filter behind every product decision.

UNWIND

Every product, design and content decision should help guests disconnect from everyday stress and reconnect with themselves and the people they are travelling with.

If a feature, photo or piece of copy doesn't support that feeling, we should question whether it belongs.
